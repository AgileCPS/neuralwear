# Earpiece Pinout

Colour code for the earpiece connector, ordered **Left to Right**.

| Pin | Signal          | Colour                                          |
|-----|-----------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 1   | GND             | <span style="color:#000000">Black</span>       |
| 2   | GND             | <span style="color:#000000">Black</span>       |
| 3   | Differential_N  | <span style="color:#aaaaaa">White</span>       |
| 4   | Differential_P  | <span style="color:#008000">Green</span>       |
| 5   | CommonMode      | <span style="color:#ccaa00">Yellow</span>      |
| 6   | 3.3V Power In   | <span style="color:#cc0000">Red</span>         |

> **Note:** Pin 6 supplies power to an internal power IC with an input voltage range of 2.7 V to 5.5 V (absolute maximum). This is compatible with a single-cell LiPo battery, which nominally operates between 3.4 V (discharged) and 4.2 V (fully charged), allowing it to power the earpiece directly without regulation.
